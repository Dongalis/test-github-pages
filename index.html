About Us

Young musicians,
big sound.

Big Band Storm is a youth big band that brings the energy and swing of live jazz performance to every stage. We're a group of passionate young musicians united by our love for music — from the golden age of jazz to the silver screen and beyond.

Our repertoire spans timeless jazz standards, cinematic film music from iconic soundtracks, and fresh arrangements of modern songs. While we're not a professional ensemble, we play with heart, commitment, and a whole lot of groove.

Whether it's a concert hall, a school event, or a local festival — Big Band Storm delivers a performance you won't forget.
